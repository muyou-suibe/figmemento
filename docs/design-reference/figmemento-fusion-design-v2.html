Start with the person, not the art. For someone who has everything, the leaf — nothing to dust, impossible to duplicate. For a child's room, wood art; for a couple, the canvas. If you tell us the story, we will tell you the form; it costs nothing.

One clear, well-lit photo is enough for most forms. Leaves and wood prefer simple backgrounds — every detail in the frame is a detail we carve, so a calm photo makes a calmer piece.
